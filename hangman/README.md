# Hangman

<img width="457" height="522" alt="hangmanSShot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8dd03886-af4f-49d5-806d-1e1b6636eb6e" />


A version of hangman to play on the computer or mobile browser.

## Words
+ The first 150 pokémon

## How to play
+ Choose a letter until you reveal the word
+ If the letter is not in the word the stickman will start getting added to the gallows
+ Try to reveal the whole word without having the entire stickman hanging
