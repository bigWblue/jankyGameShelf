# Rock Paper Scissors, minus One

An online version of the game variant of Rock Paper Scissors [played in Squid Game](https://youtu.be/AkFTzhMuHk0).

[Click here to play](https://bigwblue.github.io/jankyGameShelf/RPSminusOne/index.html)

## How to play
+ Pick two options between Rock, Paper and Scissors, repeats are allowed
+ Click Confirm to see the options you chose at the bottom and the options chosen by the computer at the top
+ Analyze which hand of yours is better against what the computer might play and click on it
+ From then on it's normal Rock Paper Scissors:
+ + Rock beats Scissors and loses to Paper
+ + Scissors beats Paper and loses to Rock
+ + Paper beats Rock and loses to Scissors
+ + If both are the same it's a tie
