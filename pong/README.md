A version of the classic Pong game using the canvas API.

<a href="https://bigwblue.github.io/jankyGameShelf/pong/index.html" target="_blank">Click here to play</a>

## How to play
+ Make the ball hit the paddle to defend your side
+ Try to get the ball past the computer paddle to score
+ The first to reach 10 points wins
